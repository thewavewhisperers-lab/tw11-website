Before buying software, fix the inputs.

A beautifully formatted report is not much use if the underlying transaction history is incomplete. These are the three steps that matter first.

STEP 01

Collect everything

Export records from each exchange. Include deposits, withdrawals, swaps, trading fees, staking activity and wallet-to-wallet transfers where applicable.

STEP 02

Reconcile the history

Check duplicates, missing transfers, unmatched deposits, fees and opening balances. Automated imports need review; they are not a guarantee of accuracy.

STEP 03

Test before upgrading

Confirm that your exchanges, transaction count, reporting country and required reports are actually supported. Compare the current plans directly.
